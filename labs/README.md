# Labs

Guided, multi-step lab notebooks that work through specific modeling tasks from
the book — fitting, sensitivity analysis, and stochastic simulation — designed
for classroom or self-study use.

**These materials are in preparation** and will be added here as they are
finalized. The book is currently under review, and the labs are being completed
alongside it.

In the meantime, the figure-reproduction scripts under
[`../notebooks/`](../notebooks/) carry the core computations each lab builds on.
